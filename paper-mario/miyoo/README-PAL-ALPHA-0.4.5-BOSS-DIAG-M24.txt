Paper Mario PAL Alpha 0.4.5 Boss Diagnostic M2.4-A

The TIMELINE diagnostic arms a bounded capture of one EVS_InitVines update.
End the timeline with the existing stop control or wait for its timeout. After
the session ends, read the M24A lines in its traces/trace_NNNNNN/trace.log.
A complete M24A record means only that the four position events were recorded
in order in the same script instance. It does not mean the full M2.4 renderer
investigation is complete. Incomplete records include a reason. Node matrices
are the actual fixed-point model matrices converted to floats, in row order.

Build this dedicated diagnostic with PM64_BOSS_DIAG_M24=1 when generating
the Miyoo build graph. Its metadata then identifies this M2.4 app and build.
No renderer geometry or rasterization is captured yet.
Data and traces remain in this app folder. Existing saves are not migrated.
